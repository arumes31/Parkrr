# Product roadmap workbench

Mode: Operate / Plan. Audience: Parkrr product owner, maintainers, and operators. Job: turn the 36 current-state recommendations into a small, inspectable delivery plan without changing production data or inventing authoritative workflow state. Primary task: understand the recommended delivery waves, search, filter, inspect, shortlist, order, annotate, deep-link, and export roadmap items. All planning state remains local to this browser.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A product roadmap is a dispatch board, not a wall of idea cards: a compact control rail narrows the field, a numbered register preserves the recommendation set, and one decision dossier explains what to do and why.

OWN-WORLD: Extend Parkrr's existing standalone workbench language: ink-green operational surfaces, garage-plan geometry, local Hanken/Bricolage/JetBrains type, restrained teal, and amber used only for genuinely deferred work. Keep the surface flat, crisp, information-dense, and free of external dependencies.

STORY: The user first sees the whole opportunity portfolio, understands four recommended delivery waves plus the trigger-based backlog, filters to the slice that matters, opens one recommendation for its evidence and implementation shape, then builds and exports a deliberately ordered shortlist.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A concise thesis and transparent snapshot lead into category and delivery-wave summaries, then a nine-step priority line and a three-part workbench: filters, dense recommendation register, and decision dossier. The signature interaction is selecting a delivery wave to filter and optionally shortlist its complete scope; mobile turns the dossier into a focused full-screen layer.

FORM: Master-detail roadmap dispatch board. It reuses Parkrr's proven development-artifact structure while changing the information model from risk triage to product prioritization. The 36-item source order remains visible and reversible; benefit and effort are explicit filters rather than an opaque score.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
